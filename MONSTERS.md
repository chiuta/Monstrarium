## 1. Bocan

_Tower of three tubes, with an antenna and egg-carton teeth_

- Difficulty: easy · 15 min
- 🧺 **All materials:** 4× Bottle caps, 3× Cardboard tubes, 2× Ice-lolly sticks or paper straws, 1× Wool, string, thick thread, 1× Egg cartons, 1× Old newspapers
- Plus (shared): White PVA glue, Masking tape, Round-tip scissors
- Parts:
  - Body: 3× Cardboard tubes
  - Eyes: 3× Bottle caps
  - Limbs: 2× Ice-lolly sticks or paper straws
  - Antennae: Wool, string, thick thread + Bottle caps
  - Teeth: Egg cartons
  - Skin or fur: Old newspapers
- Trick: stands up on its own
